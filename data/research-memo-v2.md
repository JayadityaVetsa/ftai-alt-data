# FTAI: customer demand, production and aviation

Research release: October 5, 2026. Investment horizon: October 5, 2027. Observations, guidance and model assumptions remain separate.

The strongest two pitch investigations are executable customer power shortages and the production capacity to serve Power while preserving aviation earnings.

## Customer power demand

Dominion discloses 45 GW of data-center requests without assigned energization dates and 25 GW assigned through 2031 (May report, p. 2). This supports a large planning bottleneck; it does not establish 45 GW of ready-to-buy turbine demand or a shortage duration. PJM lowered its 2027 system summer-peak forecast by 3,735 MW versus its previous forecast. These overlapping utility/system series are not added. [Dominion report](https://sustainability.dominionenergy.com/GS-5%20Large%20Load%20Rate%20Class%20Report.pdf), [PJM revision](https://insidelines.pjm.com/pjms-updated-20-year-forecast-continues-to-see-significant-long-term-load-growth/).

Seven cases separate load, IT/electrical boundaries, delivery windows, utility service, permits, gas and competition. Microsoft/Crusoe's new Abilene campus has two 336 MW IT buildings and 900 MW on-site generation announced, with the first building targeted for mid-2027. Crusoe's separate Bergen procurement has 438 MW contracted plus 310 MW LOI, phased through 2027 across multiple sites. FTAI separately has its anonymous cloud-provider anchor order. None is attributed to a named FTAI customer without evidence. [Microsoft campus](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-announces-new-900-mw-ai-factory-campus-in-abilene-texas-to-support-microsoft-ai-infrastructure), [Bergen contract](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/bergen-engines-signs-approx-750mw-u-s-power-agreement-with-crusoe-for-ai-data-centers).

Controls matter: Jupiter selects Bloom fuel cells, Lighthouse specifies 902 MW IT with utility supply, original Abilene has existing delivered capacity and backup turbines, and Saline's 1.4 GW is a January–December 2027 ramp rather than an eleven-month shortage. Leesburg is a partial utility-filing lead whose current scope could not be fully reviewed.

The calculator converts phased IT load using assumed PUE, subtracts available supply, accounts for separate competitor/capture assumptions and derating/reserve units, and caps output by allocated production. The commissionable interval begins after all readiness gates. Missing dates remain unavailable. No case yet establishes an execution-qualified open Mod-1 shortage with matched primary permission and firm fuel. Falsification includes earlier grid service, alternative procurement, customer delay, restricted primary operation or unavailable gas.

## Production while preserving aviation

FTAI module output grew from 184 in Q2 2025 to 296 in Q2 2026. Management claims 3,000 modules/year at full utilization, including partnerships, with a 1,700-module 2027 outlook. The 650+ engine production-support claim describes shared maintenance capability, not a verified dedicated Power line. [FTAI supplement, slides 13, 20 and endnotes 29](https://ir.ftaiaviation.com/static-files/a8601ed2-7367-4a33-898f-5163eb8b212d).

The starting 2027 scenario assumes 200 eligible candidates, 70% yield, 85% operational realization, 1,700 equivalents reserved for aviation, three processing equivalents per Power output, 25% shared-engine allocation, 120 dedicated tests/packages at full readiness and three start-up months. Its bottleneck supports 102 outputs, capped at the 100-unit target; it needs 143 candidates and approximately 9.4 monthly outputs after start-up. The 2028 preset targets 150 as a team scenario. These are conditional calculations, not empirical probabilities or a disclosed Mod-1 BOM.

Consolidated inventory increased from $1,193.773m in December 2025 to $1,544.592m in June 2026: $350.819m / 29.4%. FTAI separately identifies $46m H1 Power inventory working-capital use. This supports investment activity, not a precise usable-core quantity.

## Aviation cross-check and earnings expectations

FTAI Aerospace Products EBITDA rose from $164.864m in Q2 2025 to $249.716m in Q2 2026; revenue reached $875.028m. Safran independently reported 27.9% H1 civil parts revenue growth, largely CFM56-driven but also affected by LEAP, workscope and comparison base. Its February outlook adds over 750 cumulative CFM56 shop visits in 2025–2028 versus CMD2024, with decline expected from 2029. Low retirements support aviation but can make cheap Power feedstock harder to acquire. [Safran results](https://www.safran-group.com/pressroom/safran-reports-its-first-half-2026-results-2026-07-28), [Safran outlook, slide 20](https://www.safran-group.com/download/media/450393).

FTAI already published FY2027 guidance of $1.4bn Aerospace Products, $450m Power and $450m Aviation Leasing Adjusted EBITDA. These are management targets, not consensus. At 100 units, $30m costs and $1.2m non-turbine profit, an illustrative $450m operating target needs $4.788m turbine gross contribution/unit, or 79.8% of an assumed $6m ASP. Reconcile service, packaging, JV interest and transfer pricing; the simplified site proxy has different scope. [Dated guidance release](https://ftandi.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ftai-aviation-ltd-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-increases).

A single quote-provider observation is $164.70, latest trade October 6 00:15 UTC / October 5 local time; independent page corroboration was rate-limited. Reverse valuation makes other segment values and multiples explicit. No sell-side consensus or underpricing conclusion is established from capacity arithmetic alone.

## 2027–28 supply-gap focus

GE Vernova reported 116 GW of gas equipment backlog plus reserved slots in Q2 2026, against planned annual output of 20 GW starting Q3 2026 and 24 GW in 2028. These global scopes support supply pressure, not a measured US hyperscaler shortage. Site-level grid dates, phased electrical demand and on-time competitor allocations determine the accessible gap. The updated calculator shows the qualified market needed to absorb a 100-unit ramp under explicit capture, derating and reserve assumptions.

Jereh’s employer-posted sourcing role specifically covers turbine equipment, repairs, supplier qualification and delivery/capacity management. Its official careers page displays six roles with July 2025 labels. No matched historical requisition snapshots establish hiring acceleration. FTAI Power Technician mirrors remain secondary leads. Two inventory images were spot-checked; full reconciliation is outstanding.

Aircraft age above 25 years is a screening indicator, not a blanket engine service cutoff or conversion approval. FAA LLP requirements concern approved operating limits and records. Power must exceed the net present value of continued aviation or parts recovery after yield, conversion, testing and working capital.
